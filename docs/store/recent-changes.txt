When you create a database, SailVault now rates the master password: a bar, a word from Poor to Excellent and the estimated bits. It uses the same estimator as KeePassXC (zxcvbn), so both say the same about a password.

A master password that is too easy to guess is refused, also when it is long enough. "passwordpassword" has 16 characters and is still a bad idea.

The estimator knows English words only. A password made of words in another language is rated better than it is.
